FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got questions? We’ve got answers.

Do I need technical knowledge?

No advanced technical knowledge is required for the agreed setup. We include an onboarding session and show you how your workflow is intended to be used.

Will it work with my existing tools?

That depends on the tools you use and the integrations they support. We review compatibility before agreeing on the setup, so you know what can and cannot be connected before work begins.

Is €299 a subscription?

No. The Beta Setup is currently offered as a €299 one-time setup. Any future paid service or additional work would be discussed and agreed separately.

Is CashFlowLab suitable for every business?

No. We first review how you receive, manage and follow up with leads. If the setup is not a good fit for your business or tools, we’ll tell you before you commit.

Does AI contact my customers by itself?

Not by default. CashFlowLab is designed around human-controlled workflows. The exact level of automation is agreed before setup.

Do you guarantee more sales?

No. CashFlowLab is designed to improve the structure used to manage and follow up with leads. Sales results depend on many factors outside the system, so we do not guarantee revenue, conversions or specific business outcomes.
